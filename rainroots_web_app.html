Rain Gardens

Nature-based solutions for urban flooding.

What is a Rain Garden?

A rain garden is a depressed land area that collects rain water from roofs, driveways, or streets and allows it to soak into the ground. Planted with deep-rooted native plants, they act as natural sponges.

How They Help Chelsea

By capturing runoff from impermeable concrete and asphalt, rain gardens reduce the massive volume of water rushing into Chelsea's storm drains during storms. This mitigates urban flooding and filters out pollutants before they reach the Mystic River and Chelsea Creek.

Get Involved!

Ready to make a difference? Join your neighbors in building and maintaining rain gardens across Chelsea. Greenroots leads community workshops, volunteer planting days, and local environmental advocacy efforts.
